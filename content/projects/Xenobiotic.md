The catalase enzyme is inactivated by the use of xenobiotic compounds. Used PYMOL and PYRX to ascertain the degree of binding of ligand and 1TGU protein by relying on molecular docking. Results of PYRX screening : 12 ligands with binding affinity of more than -10Kcal/mol out of 1800 examined.
